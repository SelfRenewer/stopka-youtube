## Description (en)
<!-- limit:4000 -->
Found an interesting video but no time to watch it? Click ＋ in the corner of its thumbnail — it goes onto your stack and waits.

HOW IT WORKS
• The ＋ button appears on every video thumbnail on youtube.com: the home page, search, recommendations next to a video, the short-video shelf, playlists and channel pages.
• The same button sits next to the title of the video you’re watching.
• Click again to remove the video from the stack. Saved videos show a check mark.

THE LIST
• The Stopka icon in the Safari toolbar opens everything you’ve saved — with thumbnail, title and channel.
• Search by title and channel.
• “Copy links” puts all links on the clipboard — handy for sending to yourself or pasting into notes.
• Remove one video or clear them all.

PRIVACY
• The list is stored only on your Mac.
• No accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics.
• The extension works only on youtube.com and can’t see other websites.

DARK AND LIGHT
The button follows the site’s theme, the list follows your macOS appearance.

ENGLISH AND RUSSIAN
The interface follows your browser’s language.

HOW TO TURN IT ON
Open Stopka, then Safari → Settings → Extensions → turn on Stopka and allow it on youtube.com.

Stopka is an independent extension and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

## App Review Notes (en)
<!-- limit:4000 -->
1. SCREEN RECORDING
Attached to our reply in App Review. Recorded on a Mac running macOS 27.2: launching Stopka, turning it on in Safari Settings, saving videos on youtube.com with the ＋ button, opening the list, copying links and removing items.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Stopka is a Safari web extension for people who browse YouTube and want to set videos aside to watch, share or send later. It adds a ＋ button to every video thumbnail on youtube.com; one click saves the video's link, title and channel to a list. The Stopka toolbar button shows the list: search, copy all links to the clipboard at once, remove one video or clear all.
It solves a simple problem: collecting a handful of videos into one list of links quickly, without a Google account, playlists or copying URLs one by one. Everything stays on the user's Mac.

3. HOW TO SET UP AND USE
No account, login, sample files or purchases are needed.
- Open the Stopka app once, then Safari → Settings → Extensions → turn on Stopka.
- Open youtube.com and allow Stopka on this website (Stopka toolbar button → Always Allow on This Website).
- Every video thumbnail has a ＋ button in its lower-left corner. Click it — it turns into a check mark and the video is saved.
- Click the Stopka toolbar button to see the list. “Copy links” copies all links; “×” removes one video; “Clear” (press twice) removes all.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. Stopka has no server, account system, analytics, ads, third-party SDKs, AI services or payments. The list is stored locally in the extension's browser storage and is never transmitted. The extension runs only on youtube.com pages the user opens in Safari; it reads the title and channel of a saved video from that page. Thumbnails in the list are loaded by URL from YouTube's public image server (i.ytimg.com), the same images the user already sees on YouTube.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions. The interface is in English and Russian and follows the browser language.

6. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Stopka does not include, download, copy or redistribute any third-party content: no video or audio is downloaded or saved, and it does not hide, block or change YouTube's content or ads — it only adds its own ＋ button to thumbnails. It keeps links, titles and thumbnail URLs of videos the user chose, like bookmarks. Stopka is an independent extension, not affiliated with YouTube or Google, and does not operate in a regulated industry.

## Описание (ru)
<!-- limit:4000 -->
Попалось интересное видео, а смотреть некогда? Нажми ＋ в углу превью — оно ляжет в стопку и подождёт.

КАК ЭТО РАБОТАЕТ
• Кнопка ＋ появляется на каждом превью на youtube.com: на главной, в поиске, в рекомендациях рядом с видео, на полке коротких роликов, в плейлистах и на страницах каналов.
• Такая же кнопка стоит рядом с названием открытого видео.
• Нажми ещё раз — видео уберётся из стопки. Сохранённые отмечены галочкой.

СПИСОК
• Значок «Стопки» на панели Safari открывает всё, что ты отложил, — с картинкой, названием и каналом.
• Поиск по названию и каналу.
• «Скопировать ссылки» кладёт все ссылки в буфер обмена — удобно отправить себе или вставить в заметки.
• Убрать одно видео или очистить всё.

ПРИВАТНОСТЬ
• Список хранится только на твоём Маке.
• Никаких аккаунтов, серверов, рекламы и аналитики.
• Расширение работает только на youtube.com и не видит другие сайты.

ТЁМНАЯ И СВЕТЛАЯ ТЕМА
Кнопка подстраивается под тему сайта, список — под оформление macOS.

РУССКИЙ И АНГЛИЙСКИЙ
Интерфейс говорит на языке браузера.

КАК ВКЛЮЧИТЬ
Открой «Стопку», затем Safari → Настройки → Расширения → включи «Стопку» и разреши ей youtube.com.

«Стопка» — независимое расширение, не связанное с YouTube и Google.
